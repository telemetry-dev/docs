A score is one result on a trace, a span, a session, or an experiment item. Scores come from [evaluators](/evals/evaluators), from reviewers in [review queues](/evals/review-queues), from members on the trace page, and from your app.

## Kinds of scores

| Kind | Values | Shown as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pass / fail | The label `pass` with the value 1, or `fail` with 0 | **Pass** or **Fail** |
| Number | A number in the range of the evaluator, such as 1 to 5 | The number, such as "4 / 5" |
| Labels | One label of the evaluator | The label |
| Up / down | 1 for up, 0 for down | A thumb with **Up** or **Down** |

A score can also show one of these statuses:

- **Not scored**: the evaluator did not apply, such as a code check that returned `null`, or a trace from an environment with prompt capture off.
- **Judge error**: the evaluator returned an error, such as a provider error.

Pass rates and averages count only the scores with a value. They do not count **Not scored** and **Judge error**.

## Read scores on a trace

Open a trace and select its root span. The **Scores** section of the span panel shows the scores of the trace and of the span. A different span shows the section only when it has scores of its own.

Each row shows the name of the score, the value, the source, and the reason or the comment. The source is one of these:

- The model of an LLM judge, such as "gpt-4o-mini judge".
- **Code check** or **Rule**.
- The reviewer of a review queue, such as "Maya Chen · review queue".
- The name of the member who added the score on the trace page.
- **From your app**, for a score from the SDK or the API.

## Add a score on a trace

Members can add a human score on a trace. The evaluator must be a human score with **Members on Trace detail** selected. Read [Human scores](/evals/evaluators#human-scores).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add score">
    Open the trace, then select **Add score** in the **Scores** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give the score">
    Select the **Score**, then the **Value**. Enter an optional **Comment** of up to 4,000 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the score">
    Select **Save score**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The score goes on the span that you look at. If you add the same score again on the same span, your new score replaces your earlier score.

Only the person who added a score on the trace page can remove it. To remove it, select **Remove score** in its row. You cannot remove scores from evaluators, review queues, or your app.

## Read scores on a session

The session page shows the newest score of each name on the session. These include the scores that your app sent with only a session ID, and the reviews of the session. Each turn shows a summary of the scores of its trace.

## Filter traces by a score

The trace list on the **Traces** page has a **Scores** column. Each cell shows one score and its value, and **+N** for the other scores of the trace. Hover over **+N** to see the names of all of them. A trace can have more than one pass or fail score of the same name, such as from two reviewers. The cell then shows the pass rate.

To filter by a score, select a value in the **Scores** column, such as **Fail**. A chip above the table shows the filter, such as "Correctness Fail". Select the chip to clear the filter. You can filter by a pass or fail value, an up or down value, or a label.

The filter has these limits:

- It reads the newest 1,000 scores that match. When more scores match, the list says that traces with older scores do not show.
- It counts only scores with a value.
- It leaves out experiment runs, unless the **Environment** filter is `experiment`.
- The **Spans** view has no score filter.

A score from your app with a name that no evaluator has shows its number or label, and you cannot filter by it.

## Send scores from your app

Use `score` to record a score on a trace, a span, or a session. For example, send the thumbs up or down of a user on a reply:

<CodeGroup>

```ts TypeScript
import { score, startActiveSpan } from "@telemetry-dev/sdk";

// When the app replies, keep the trace ID of the reply with the message.
const reply = await startActiveSpan("reply", async (span) => ({
  text: await replyToCustomer(message),
  traceId: span.traceId,
}));

// Later, when the user selects thumbs down:
await score({ name: "user_feedback", traceId: reply.traceId, value: 0, comment: "Wrong amount" });
```

```python Python
import telemetry_dev

# When the app replies, keep the trace ID of the reply with the message.
with telemetry_dev.start_span("reply") as span:
    text = reply_to_customer(message)
    trace_id = f"{span.span.get_span_context().trace_id:032x}"

# Later, when the user selects thumbs down:
telemetry_dev.score(name="user_feedback", trace_id=trace_id, value=0, comment="Wrong amount")
```

</CodeGroup>

`score` is in `@telemetry-dev/sdk` 0.2.2 or later, and in `telemetry-dev` 0.3.2 or later. The API key must have **Run experiments** access. Call `score` from your server. Do not put the key in a browser.

- Give `traceId` (`trace_id`), `sessionId` (`session_id`), or the two. To score one span, also give its `spanId` (`span_id`).
- Give `value`, `label`, or the two. A pass or fail score has the label `pass` with the value 1, or `fail` with 0.
- `user_feedback` takes the value 1 for up or 0 for down, and no label. The first `user_feedback` score of a project adds the **User feedback** evaluator.
- A score can have a `comment` of up to 4,000 characters. telemetry.dev applies the [redaction](/platform/privacy#what-built-in-redaction-removes) of the project to the comment.

`score` does not throw errors into your code. It returns the ID of the score, or `undefined` (`None` in Python) after it reports a `ScoreError`. The error goes to the `onError` (`on_error`) of the client, or to the SDK log when no client is initialized. It makes one request, with no retry. In TypeScript, it waits 10 seconds at most. In Python, `score_async` runs the request in a worker thread.

Each call adds a new score. telemetry.dev does not merge a score with an earlier score of the same name. It also does not examine whether the trace exists.

To send a score from a different language, use `POST /v1/scores`. Read [Send a score](/reference/evals-api#send-a-score).

## Get an alert when a pass rate falls

The **Eval score drop** alert sends an email when the pass rate of a pass or fail evaluator over 6 hours becomes less than a limit. Read [Eval score drop](/platform/alerts#eval-score-drop).

## Scores of prompt versions

The **Metrics** tab of a prompt shows two scores for each version of the prompt: the pass rate of a live pass or fail evaluator, and the **User feedback**. Versions newer than the `production` version show the change from it. The tab counts only scores of live traces, not of experiment runs.

## Retention

Scores of traces and sessions follow the retention of the project. When retention deletes a trace, telemetry.dev also deletes its scores. A score without a trace, such as a score of a session, expires by the time that telemetry.dev got it. Scores of experiment items stay with their experiment. Read [Set retention](/platform/privacy#set-retention).
