Members of an organization can use all of its projects. To manage members, open the organization switcher in the sidebar, select **Organization settings**, and then select **Members** in the settings sidebar.

## Roles

An organization member has one of three roles:

| Role | What the role can do |
| --- | --- |
| `member` | View telemetry, usage, settings, and the API key list. Create projects. |
| `admin` | Everything a member can do. Also invite and manage members, create and revoke API keys, change project names, privacy settings, and retention, delete projects, rename the organization, manage alert rules, and change the plan. |
| `owner` | Everything an admin can do. Also give the `owner` role, manage other owners, and delete the organization. |

An admin can give the `member` or `admin` role, and cannot change or remove an owner. Only an owner can give the `owner` role. The member table does not let you change your own role.

## Invite a member

An owner or admin can invite people.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open members">
    Open **Organization settings**, then select **Members** in the settings sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the invitation">
    Select **Invite member**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Enter the **Email** address and select a **Role**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Select **Send invitation**. telemetry.dev emails an invitation link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An invitation expires 48 hours after it is sent.

The person opens the link and selects **Accept invitation** or **Decline**. They must sign in with the invited email address and verify it before they can see the invitation.

### Seats

Each member and each unexpired pending invitation uses one seat.

| Plan | Seats |
| --- | ---: |
| Free | 2 |
| Pro | 5 |
| Team | Unlimited |

When every seat is in use, **Invite member** is turned off. If two people send invitations for the last seat at the same moment, telemetry.dev accepts one and rejects the other. To free a seat, revoke a pending invitation or remove a member. To add seats, read [Change your plan](/platform/billing#change-your-plan).

## Manage pending invitations

**Pending invitations** lists each invitation's email address, role, and expiry time. Each row has these buttons:

- **Copy invite link**: copy the link to send it yourself, for example if the email did not arrive.
- **Resend invitation**: send the email again. Owners and admins only.
- **Revoke invitation**: cancel the invitation and free its seat. Owners and admins only.

## Change a member's role

Select a new role in the member's row. The role limits above still apply.

## Remove a member

Select the delete button in the member's row, then select **Remove** to confirm. The person loses access to the organization and its projects.

## Leave an organization

Open **Organization settings**, which starts on the **General** page. Under **Danger zone**, select **Leave** in the **Leave organization** row, then select **Leave** again to confirm. telemetry.dev takes you to onboarding, where you can accept another invitation or create an organization.

If you are the only owner, you cannot leave. Give another member the `owner` role first, or select **Transfer ownership** on the same page. Transferring makes the other member the owner and you an admin.
