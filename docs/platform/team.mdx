Open **Organization settings → Members** to manage access to the organization and its projects.

## Roles

An organization has three roles:

| Role | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Member | View telemetry, usage, and privacy settings. Create projects. |
| Admin | Member access, plus project settings, API keys, privacy, retention, and organization settings. |
| Owner | Admin access, plus the ability to assign the owner role and change other owners. |

An admin can assign the `member` or `admin` role. Only an owner can assign the `owner` role. An admin cannot change another owner. An owner cannot change their own role from the member table.

## Invite a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open members">
    Open **Organization settings → Members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start an invitation">
    Select **Invite member**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set access">
    Enter the email address. Then select a role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Submit the invitation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An admin can invite a member or admin. An owner can also invite an owner.

The seat count includes organization members and active pending invitations. At the plan limit, the invite button is not available. The server also rejects an invitation if a concurrent invitation takes the last seat.

| Plan | Seats |
| --- | ---: |
| Free | 2 |
| Pro | 5 |
| Team | Unlimited |

Read [Plans and billing](/platform/billing) to change the seat limit.

## Manage pending invitations

The pending invitation table shows the email address, role, and expiration. Use its action menu to:

- **Copy link** for direct delivery
- **Resend** the invitation email
- **Revoke** the invitation

A new member can accept or decline the invitation. telemetry.dev asks an unverified account to complete email verification before it shows the invitation.

## Change or remove a member

Use the role menu in the member row to change access. Role limits still apply to owners and admins.

Select **Remove member** to revoke a member's organization and project access. Confirm the removal in the dialog.

## Leave an organization

Select **Leave organization** in the Members tab, then confirm the action. telemetry.dev removes the active organization and sends you to onboarding.
