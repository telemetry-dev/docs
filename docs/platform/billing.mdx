Each organization has one plan. The plan sets project, seat, retention, and monthly ingestion-unit limits.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Projects | Seats | Retention | Ingestion units per month |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Free | $0 | 1 | 2 | 7 days | 10,000 |
| Pro | $25 per month | 5 | 5 | 30 days | 250,000 |
| Team | $100 per month | Unlimited | Unlimited | 90 days | 1,000,000 |

For the latest purchase terms, read [telemetry.dev/pricing](https://telemetry.dev/pricing).

## Ingestion units

One ingestion unit is one span, one log record, or one metric data point.

The limit belongs to the organization, not to one project. Usage resets at the first day of each UTC month.

### Hard quota behavior

The Free plan has a 1.2 hard-quota factor. Its 10,000-unit plan limit becomes a hard stop at 12,000 units.

After the hard stop, the ingest service rejects more data with `429 quota_exceeded`. The response has a `Retry-After` value for the next UTC month boundary.

At this time, paid plans record usage but do not reject ingestion at the listed monthly limit.

## Monitor usage

Open **Organization settings → Usage** to read:

- Ingestion units used and the plan limit
- Spans in the current month
- Projects used and the plan limit
- Seats used and the plan limit
- The next reset time

The seat count includes members and active pending invitations.

Open **Project settings → Usage** for one project's ingestion units and span count. This tab also shows retained traces, spans, logs, and metric points. The monthly plan limit still applies across the full organization.

## Change a plan

An organization owner or admin can change the plan.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open billing">
    Open **Organization settings → Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a plan">
    Select the Pro or Team plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete checkout">
    Complete the Polar checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to telemetry.dev">
    After checkout, telemetry.dev refreshes the organization plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage a subscription

Select **Manage billing** to open the Polar customer portal. The admin whose Polar customer owns the subscription gets this action.

A different admin sees that another organization admin manages billing. That admin cannot open the subscription portal for the purchaser.
